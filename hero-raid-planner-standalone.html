Supported equipment and movement rules

Weapons, shields, breastplates, segmented armour, mounts and spurs use the hero page's final strength/speed. Damage reduction, XP helmets and regeneration equipment use their numeric active benefits. Small Map / Map / Large Map give +30% / +40% / +50% return speed only: a one-hour outward journey with Small Map returns in 1 / 1.3 hours, not 0.7 hours.

Distance boots and Tournament Square apply only beyond 20 fields, with multiplier 1 + 0.2 × TS level + boots bonus / 100. Pennants, standards, Natar horns, thief bags, troop training/weapon bonuses, culture points and resource production do not improve a solo oasis raid. Inventory consumables are not used by the planner. Unknown active benefits are flagged, not silently ignored.

Official references: left-hand items, armour, helmets and boots, movement, health.

4 · The balance dial

0 — only free hits50 — balanced100 — res/hour at any cost
One dial, three things. Ranking is net/h = (bounty − P × HP) / round-trip hours — resources on top, health priced at P, time in the divisor. Sliding greed just moves P: at 100 health is free so it chases raw res/hour; at 0 health is priceless so it only takes hits your armour absorbs. 50 sits on the shadow price — what a point of health is actually worth given how much of it you have to spend. The HP floor and per-trip HP cap above are planning preferences, not hero stats. Raids only, one visit per oasis. All targets remain visible regardless of their resource rate.
