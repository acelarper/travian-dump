2 · Paste the map data

🔒 This page makes no network requests on its own. Nothing polls, nothing runs on a timer, nothing is sent anywhere. It only acts when you click a button, and then only to localhost.
  1. Press F12 → Network tab, then open the map page below.
  2. In the filter box type position.
  3. Click the position request → Response tab → select all → copy.
  4. Paste it in the box below and press Analyse.
The map page already made this exact request to draw the tiles. You are just reading the answer it got — your account sends nothing extra at all.

Bounty = crop supply × 160 per animal killed (40 of each resource, straight into your hero's inventory — not limited by carry capacity, and paid even if every attacker dies). Profit = credited scan bounty − planned losses × 1,090. Both rate columns use that profit and the full round-trip time, including Tournament Square. The arrow in Loss est. shows a tougher refill, not an extra charge in the displayed profit. Larger escorts that reduce that risk stay available, without forcing every send to cover all species spawning at once. Only kills from the scanned population are credited; additional animals never fund the raid. Credited bounty must cover at least 1.5× the modelled replacement cost. Hospital treatment and retraining still take resources and time, so no recovery discount is assumed. Empty oases are not listed at all — they hold no animals, and their stored production is not what a Thunder fleet is for.
